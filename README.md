# EDCI 59100-016 - Literature Review Methodologies

Public archive staging for Summer 2026 Purdue EDCI 59100-016 coursework and literature-review manuscript development.

## Overview

This course focused on literature-based research, literature review methodologies, and manuscript development for publication. The local course work culminated in a scoping-review manuscript on vision-language models for construction site safety and progress monitoring.

This staging repo preserves the course sequence, assignment outputs, manuscript build materials, and review corpus metadata. It also includes the review-paper PDFs locally because that was requested, but public pushing of third-party publisher PDFs should wait until redistribution rights are confirmed.

## Folder Guide

### `00_Admin/`

Course-level material, including the syllabus, quiz note, and Purdue OWL writing guidelines used during the course.

### `01_Weekly_Coursework/`

The course organized by week. This includes lecture notes, assignment instructions, submissions, instructor or writing-lab feedback, drafts, sample solutions, and final manuscript build materials.

Major assignment coverage:

- Week 1: methodology comparison and topic exploration.
- Week 2: research topic/methodology justification and literature review planning artifacts.
- Week 3: source evaluation matrix and final literature review plan artifacts.
- Week 4: writing-lab reflection, synthesis/outline working material, and reviewed drafts.
- Week 5: writing-lab reflection, draft manuscript, working reference list, and peer-review draft material.
- Week 6: final revised literature review manuscript plus build/data audit scripts.

### `02_Review_Papers/`

The local review corpus used for the manuscript and assignment work. These PDFs are included in local staging because the user requested them, but this folder should not be pushed publicly unless public redistribution rights are confirmed.

### `99_Tracking/`

Contains the complete manifest, review-paper manifest, missing-content notes, and huge-file/exclusion decisions.

## Final Manuscript

The final manuscript is staged under:

`01_Weekly_Coursework/Week 6/04-Final-Manuscript-Working/`

It includes `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.tex`, generated figure assets, and build/data scripts.

## Public Release Warning

This repo is staged locally. Before pushing to a public GitHub repository, confirm rights for:

- third-party research-paper PDFs under `02_Review_Papers/`
- extracted full-text paper notes under `Week 4/03-Assignments/Assignment8_Working/paper_text_extracts/`
- any course-provided readings or sample solutions not authored by the user

Lecture/media videos are excluded from this staging repo by decision.
