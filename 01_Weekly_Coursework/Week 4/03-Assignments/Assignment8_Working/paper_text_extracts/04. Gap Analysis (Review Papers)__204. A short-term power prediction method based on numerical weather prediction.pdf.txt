A short-term power prediction method based on numerical weather
prediction correction and the fusion of adaptive spatiotemporal graph
feature information for wind farm cluster

A R T I C L E I N F O                                     A B S T R A C T

Keywords:                                                 In recent years, with the construction of new electricity system, the scale of wind power grid connection has been
Wind power prediction                                     continuously expanding. Accurate wind power prediction is crucial for ensuring the stable operation of the power
NWP wind speed correction                                 system. However, the existing short-term power prediction methods for wind farm cluster (WFC) have short-
Adaptive spatiotemporal graph
                                                          comings in extracting spatiotemporal features, making it difficult to achieve high-precision prediction. Therefore,
Deep learning
                                                          this paper proposes a WFC short-term power prediction method based on numerical weather prediction (NWP)
                                                          correction and the information fusion of adaptive spatiotemporal graph feature for WFC. Firstly, similar infor-
                                                          mation between future and past periods of NWP is sought to achieve NWP wind speed correction by the defined
                                                          error correlation ratio. Then, the corrected NWP wind speed is combined with the information of geographical
                                                          location of the wind farms, and the adaptive spatiotemporal graph network is used to achieve short-term wind
                                                          power prediction of the WFC. The proposed method is applied to two large-scale WFCs in China. The experi-
                                                          mental results show that compared with other comparative algorithms, the RMSE is reduced by 0.0100 averagely
                                                          and the MAE is reduced by 0.0096 averagely. The coefficient of determination is improved 0.0440 averagely and
                                                          the accuracy rate is improved 1.01% averagely. The above results indicate that the predicted power obtained by
                                                          the proposed method is closest to the actual power. It is beneficial for improving the utilization efficiency of wind
                                                          power and reducing the pressure on power system scheduling.
