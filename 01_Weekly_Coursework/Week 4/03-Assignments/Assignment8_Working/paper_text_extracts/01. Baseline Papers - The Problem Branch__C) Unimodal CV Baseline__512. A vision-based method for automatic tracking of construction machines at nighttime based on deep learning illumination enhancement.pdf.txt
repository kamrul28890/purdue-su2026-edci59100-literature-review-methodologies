Deep learning                                            to low lighting conditions and fatiguing environments. Therefore, this study proposes a vision-based method
Image enhancement                                        specifically for automatic tracking of construction machines at nighttime by integrating the deep learning illu­
Construction machines

Nighttime construction

                                                         enhancement, machine detection, Kalman filter tracking, machine association, and linear assignment. Then, a
                                                         testing experiment based on nine nighttime videos is conducted to evaluate the tracking performance using this
                                                         approach. The results show that the method developed in this study achieved 95.1% in MOTA and 75.9% in

                                                         automated surveillance tasks in nighttime construction to improve the productivity and safety performance.
