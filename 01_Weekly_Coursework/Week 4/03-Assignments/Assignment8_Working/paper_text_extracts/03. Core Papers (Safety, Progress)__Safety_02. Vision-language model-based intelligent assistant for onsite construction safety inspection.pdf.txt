3. Research method

    This section describes the development and evaluation process of the
proposed system that integrates computer vision (CV) and language
model processing (LLM) for analyzing construction scenes. The identi­
fication of the problem stemmed from a comprehensive review of the
literature, followed by the proposal and evaluation of solutions for each
identified issue.
    The system’s development phase commenced with the creation of a
CV-based model, achieved through data collection and preprocessing
techniques. Leveraging image data from publicly available datasets and
augmenting it with additional images to ensure diversity in the training
data. In the post-processing phase, safety rules relevant to objects were
integrated to classify scenes in compliance with safety regulations. This
process was adopted for three distinct scenarios.
    Textual information derived from this process underwent LLM pro­
cessing to generate descriptive captions, laying the foundation for the
subsequent interactive chatbot module. The integration of these mod­
ules culminated in the development of an Android mobile application,
tailored for ease of use by construction personnel on-site.
    Evaluation metrics were employed to assess the accuracy of the ob­
ject detection model, post-processing outcomes, and generated captions.
