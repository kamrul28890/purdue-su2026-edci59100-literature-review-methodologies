A R T I C L E I N F O                                        A B S T R A C T

Keywords:                                                    Low situational awareness contributes to safety incidents in construction. Existing Deep Learning (DL)-based
Construction safety                                          applications lack the capability to provide context-specific and interactive feedback that is essential for workers
Deep learning                                                to fully understand their surrounding environments. This paper proposes the Visual Construction Safety Query
Vision-language models
                                                             (VCSQ) system. The system encompasses real-time Image Captioning (IC), safety-centric Visual Question
Augmented reality
                                                             Answering (VQA), and keyword-based Image-Text Retrieval (ITR), integrated with head-mounted Augmented
                                                             Reality (AR) devices. System validation includes benchmarks and real-world images. The ITR module posted high
                                                             recall rates of 0.801 and 0.835 for Recall@5 and @10. The VQA module achieved an 89.7% accuracy rate, and
                                                             the IC module had a SPICE score of 0.449. Feasibility tests and surveys confirmed the system's practical ad­
                                                             vantages in different construction scenarios. This study establishes an integration roadmap adaptable to future
                                                             advancements in interactive DL and immersive AR.

                                                                                    4. Implementation, experiments, and results

                                                                                        As the development in this study is a proof-of-concept prototype, the
                                                                                    range of the query system is limited to hazardous circumstances asso­
                                                                                    ciated with 7 common construction safety considerations (Table 3).
                                                                                    Each example question is directly linked to these safety considerations.
                                                                                    These questions and their respective answers will later form the basis for
                                                                                    dataset development. To broaden the scope of safety considerations
                                                                                    beyond the initial seven categories, one effective strategy could be the
                                                                                    use of LLMs like GPT-4 [31]. Utilising such models can assist in gener­
                                                                                    ating a range of questions that address diverse safety issues, thereby
                                                                                    enriching the dataset. This approach could significantly expedite the
                                                                                    task of compiling a more comprehensive and relevant set of safety
                                                                                    questions. The experiment is conducted on a server with a 3.40 GHz Intel
                                                                                    Core i7-13700KF CPU and GeForce RTX 4080 GPU with 16 GB VRAM.
                                                                                    The proposed system has been implemented with Python language. The
                                                                                    PyTorch framework was used to perform the VCSQ system.
