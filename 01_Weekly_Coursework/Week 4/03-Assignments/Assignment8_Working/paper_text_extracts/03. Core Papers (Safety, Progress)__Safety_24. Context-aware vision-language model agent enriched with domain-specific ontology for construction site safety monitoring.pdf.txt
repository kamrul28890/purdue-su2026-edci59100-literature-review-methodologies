                                                                                       Specifically, the architecture of CogAgent [55] is chosen as our
                                                                                   baseline model, with added-on LoRA adapters used for fine-tuning, as
                                                                                   illustrated in Fig. 5. CogAgent is built on a pre-trained VLM, specifically
                                                                                   the CogVLM 17B, which is an open-source and state-of-the-art large
                                                                                   vision language model.
                                                                                       EVA2-CLIP-E is used as the encoder for low-resolution image input
                                                                                   (490 × 490 pixels), and an MLP adapter to map its output into the
                                                                                   feature space of the visual-language decoder. The visual-language
                                                                                   decoder consists of both vision experts and language backbone for
                                                                                   effective deep fusion between image feature and language feature. The
                                                                                   decoder processes a combined input of the low-resolution image feature
                                                                                   sequence and text feature sequence, and autoregressively outputs the
                                                                                   target text. However, the original CogVLM can only accommodate im­
                                                                                   ages of relatively low resolution (e.g. 224 or 490), which is insufficient
                                                                                   for screen resolution of computers or smart devices which is typically
                                                                                   1280 × 720 pixels or higher. To address this, a high-resolution but size-
                                                                                   efficient cross-module is introduced in the CogAgent architecture, which
                                                                                   not only maintains efficiency with high-resolution images but also offers
                                                                                   flexible adaptability to a variety of visual-language model architectures.
                                                                                   The high-resolution cross-module acts as a new branch for higher-
                                                                                   resolution input, accepting images of size 1120 × 1120 pixels. Unlike
                                                                                   the original low-resolution input branch, the high-resolution cross-
                                                                                   module adopts a much smaller pre-trained vision encoder (EVA2-CLIP-L
                                                                                   with 0.30B parameters), and uses cross-attention of a small hidden size
                                                                                   to fuse high-resolution image features with every layer of the VLM
                                                                                   decoder, thus reducing the computational cost. The formulation of each
                                                                                   decoder layer’s attention module is summarized in Eqs. (1)–(2):
