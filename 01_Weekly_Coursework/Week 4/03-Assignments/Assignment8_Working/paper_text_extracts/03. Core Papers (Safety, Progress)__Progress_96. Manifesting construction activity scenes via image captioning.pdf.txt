Keywords:                                                 This study proposed an automated method for manifesting construction activity scenes by image captioning – an
Construction activity scenes                              approach rooted in computer vision and natural language generation. A linguistic description schema for
Computer vision                                           manifesting the scenes is developed initially and two unique dedicated image captioning datasets are created for
Natural language generation                               method validation. A general model architecture of image captioning is then instituted by combining an encoder-
Image captioning
                                                          decoder framework with deep neural networks, followed by three experimental tests involving the selection of
Convolutional neural network
Recurrent neural network

                                                          comparable with that of state-of-the-art computer vision methods in general. The paper concludes with a dis-
                                                          cussion of the feasibility of the practical application of the proposed approach at the current technical level.
