Relation extraction                                       regulations for detecting violations. Relation extraction provides an automated solution to extract relations that
Construction safety                                       describe construction safety requirements from unstructured text. However, previous relation extraction efforts
Fall protection
                                                          are limited in their extraction capabilities, representation, and automation. To address this gap, this paper
Field compliance checking
Deep learning
                                                          proposes a deep learning-based method to automatically extract and represent relations that describe fall pro­
Knowledge graphs                                          tection requirements. The proposed method: (1) uses a CNN-based model, with pre-trained word and position
Word embeddings                                           embeddings, to automatically extract domain-specific relations, and (2) represents the extracted requirements in
                                                          the form of knowledge graph-based queries, which helps decompose complex requirements into manageable
                                                          units while keeping these units connected in a scalable graph structure. The proposed method was tested on 20
                                                          OSHA sections, and has achieved 87.5% precision, 83.4% recall, and 85.4% F-1 measure, which indicates good
                                                          relation extraction performance.
