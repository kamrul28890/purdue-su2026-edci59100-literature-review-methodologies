Keywords:                                                   This paper developed and evaluated the Precast Concrete Installation Dataset (PCI-Dataset), a large-scale video
Precast concrete installation                               dataset for automatically recognizing precast concrete (PC) installation activities. The dataset comprises 12,791
Off-site construction                                       video clips (5 s each, 1080 × 1080 resolution, 30fps) from actual PC construction sites, including 12 balanced
Computer vision
                                                            activity classes combining three component types and four work stages. Evaluation of six Transformer-based
Transformer models
Video recognition
                                                            video classification models showed VideoMAE V2 achieved the highest overall accuracy of 98.10 %, followed
                                                            by UniFormer V2, Video Swin, MVIT, ViViT, and TimeSformer. VideoMAE V2 achieved F1 scores above 80 % for
                                                            most activities, with a peak of 92.20 % for slab assembly. In a case study on a real PC construction site, the model
                                                            demonstrated high recognition accuracies: 100 % for lifting, 85.83–100 % for rigging, and 93.75–100 % for
                                                            assembly operations. The paper contributes to PC construction management theory by applying computer vision
                                                            for real-time and automated work recognition and analysis.
