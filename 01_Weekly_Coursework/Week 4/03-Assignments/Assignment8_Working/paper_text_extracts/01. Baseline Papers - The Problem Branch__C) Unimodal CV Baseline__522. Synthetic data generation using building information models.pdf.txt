Synthetic data generation using building information models

School of Civil and Environmental Engineering, Yonsei University, 50, Yonsei-ro, Seodaemun-gu, Seoul 03722, Republic of Korea




A R T I C L E I N F O                                      A B S T R A C T

Keywords:                                                  Infrastructure scene understanding from image data aids diverse applications in construction and maintenance.
Automatic annotation                                       Recently, deep learning models have been employed to extract information regarding infrastructure from visual
Two-stage synthetic data generation                        data. The performance of these models depends significantly on the volume of training data. However, preparing
Building information model
                                                           the training data is time-consuming and laborious, as it entails labeling numerous images. To address this issue,
Deep learning model
Image-to-image translation
                                                           this paper proposes a method for generating high-quality synthetic data that includes the automatic annotation of
Infrastructure scene understanding                         infrastructure scenes. The method consists of three steps: 1) translating building information model (BIM) images
                                                           into real-world images, 2) automatically labeling them using the spatial information contained in the BIM to
                                                           generate various synthetic datasets, and 3) splicing the selected synthetic datasets together to form the final
                                                           synthetic dataset. The Mask R-CNN models trained with building and bridge synthetic data achieved average
                                                           precisions of 71.6% and 84.9%, respectively.
