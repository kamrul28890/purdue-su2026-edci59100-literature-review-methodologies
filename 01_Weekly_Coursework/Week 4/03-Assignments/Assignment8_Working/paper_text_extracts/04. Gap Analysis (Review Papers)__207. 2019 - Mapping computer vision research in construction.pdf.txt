Keywords:                                                 Computer vision is transforming processes associated with the engineering and management of construction
Computer vision                                           projects. It can enable the acquisition, processing, analysis of digital images, and the extraction of high-di-

                                                          struction, we performed a detailed bibliometric and scientometric analysis of the normative literature from 2000
                                                          to 2018. We identified the primary areas where computer vision has been applied, including defect inspection,
                                                          safety monitoring, and performance analysis. By performing a mapping exercise, a detailed analysis of the
                                                          computer vision literature enables the identification of gaps in knowledge, which provides a platform to support
                                                          future research in this fertile area for construction.
