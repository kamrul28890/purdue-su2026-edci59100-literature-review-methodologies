Keywords:                                                 Due to the decentralisation and complexity of knowledge in the architecture, engineering and construction (AEC)
Zero-shot learning                                        industry, the research on knowledge graphs (KGs) is still insufficient, and most of the research focuses on text-
Computer vision                                           based KG structuring or updating. Entity extraction, a sub-task of knowledge extraction, is critical in general KG
Entity extraction
                                                          update approaches. While the mainstream approach for this task generally uses textual data, visual data is more
Knowledge graph
Knowledge update
                                                          readily available, more accurate and has a shorter update cycle than textual data. Therefore, this paper integrates
                                                          zero-shot learning techniques with general KGs to present a novel framework called “video2entities” that can
                                                          extract entities from videos to update the AEC KG. The framework combines the perceptual capabilities of
                                                          computer vision with the cognitive capabilities of KG to improve the accuracy and timeliness of KG updates.
                                                          Experimental results demonstrate that the framework can extract “new entities” from architectural decoration
                                                          videos for AEC KG updates.
