## WHAT DOES GOOD LOOK LIKE?

### Assignment 1 Example (Good):
```
Article 1 - Evidence and Gap Maps Comparison

Predicted Methodology: Scoping Review

Key Evidence:
- "This paper provides a comprehensive review of different approaches to 
  evidence and gap mapping..." (p. 2)
- "We systematically compared methodologies used in 47 published gap maps..." 
  (Methods section)
- Results present categorized overview of mapping approaches rather than 
  answering a specific question

Reasoning: This is a scoping review because the primary purpose is to map the 
landscape of evidence and gap mapping approaches rather than systematically 
answer a specific research question. The search strategy is comprehensive and 
systematic, but the outcome is a descriptive categorization of approaches, 
which is characteristic of scoping reviews. The authors emphasize clarifying 
the range of methodologies available, not synthesizing evidence to answer a 
narrow question.
```

## Quick Help Reference

**Can't decide between Narrative and Integrative?**
- Narrative = What is known about X?
- Integrative = What patterns emerge across X?

**Can't decide between Scoping and Systematic?**
- Scoping = What exists? (mapping the landscape)
- Systematic = What does evidence show? (rigorous answer to specific Q)

**Topic too broad?**
- Try adding more specific criteria or population
- Example: "Education" → "How teacher PD affects online teaching effectiveness"

**Topic too narrow?**
- Try broadening the question slightly
- Example: "How X software was used at Y university in 2023" → "What barriers prevent adoption of technology X in higher education?"

**Can't find literature?**
- Try different search terms (synonyms)
- Try broader topic
- Try your library database (better than Google Scholar)
