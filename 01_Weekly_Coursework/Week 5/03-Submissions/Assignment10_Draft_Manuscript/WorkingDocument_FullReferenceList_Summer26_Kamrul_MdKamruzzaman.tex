\section*{Corpus Overview}

The total corpus for this scoping review comprises \textbf{58 peer-reviewed studies}, reconciled from the authoritative charted dataset and assembled via two parallel identification tracks. The Phase 1 database track (Web of Science and Scopus, searched 4 March 2026, followed by title/abstract and full-text screening) yielded \textbf{19 core VLM studies}, classified into Paradigms A, B, and C. The Phase 2 backward-snowballing track yielded \textbf{39 background/transition studies}, of which 5 are transformer-based transitional ``bridging'' works and 34 are pre-VLM baseline studies (sensor-based, computer vision, or NLP approaches).

Of the 34 Phase 2 baseline studies, 17 are cited individually in the manuscript; the remaining 17 appear only in aggregate as contextual background. All 42 sources individually cited in the manuscript are listed below in full bibliographic detail, organized by corpus phase and architectural paradigm. The additional 17 Phase 2 baseline papers are tracked in the Phase 2 evidence synthesis matrix from Assignment 8.

\textbf{Note on the arXiv preprint by Bui et al. (2026):} this source is \emph{not} counted in the 58-study corpus and contributes no descriptive or quantitative figures, consistent with the manuscript's peer-reviewed-only eligibility criterion. It is retained as a single, clearly flagged piece of contextual evidence in the Discussion (its pre-review status is noted wherever cited) and is listed separately below rather than under Paradigm A.
