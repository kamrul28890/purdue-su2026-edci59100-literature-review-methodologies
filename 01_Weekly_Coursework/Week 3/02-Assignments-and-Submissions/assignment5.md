Search Results & Source Evaluation Matrix

Building on your work:
This assignment brings together your work from this week’s activities and discussion. It should reflect your searching, screening, and evaluation process, including any revisions you made based on feedback.
Overview
This assignment focuses on documenting and reflecting on your process for identifying and evaluating literature.
Your goal is to demonstrate that:
You are developing an effective search strategy
You are applying inclusion/exclusion criteria
You are making thoughtful screening decisions
You are beginning to understand patterns across sources
This assignment represents your current working progress and will continue to evolve.
Instructions
Submit your Searching and Evaluating Literature working document.
Your document should include the following components:
1. Search Log
Document your search process.

Include:
Databases used
Search strings
Filters applied
Number of results
Notes on effectiveness
Show how your search strategy developed over time.

2. Screening Decisions
Include a set of sources you reviewed during screening.
For each source:
Indicate Keep / Maybe / Exclude
Provide a brief explanation of your decision
Your reasoning should reflect your inclusion/exclusion criteria.

3. Preliminary Set of Included Studies
Identify sources you are likely to include.
These may still change as your work develops.

4. Source Evaluation Matrix (Article Notes)
For selected sources, include:
Article
Main Idea
Method
Key Findings
Notes (Why it matters)
Focus on extracting key information efficiently.
